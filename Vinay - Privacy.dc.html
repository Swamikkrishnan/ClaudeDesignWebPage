Privacy & Scope

What happens to what you share.

The Alignment Call form asks only for what helps a first conversation go well: how to reach you, and a first sketch of what brought you here. It is read by Vinay alone, used to prepare for your call, and not shared with anyone else.

You are only ever asked to share what feels necessary for a first conversation. More detailed personal history can be discussed later, once trust and context make that appropriate — never before.

Submitting the form is a request for a conversation, not an automatic booking. Nothing is scheduled until you and Vinay speak.

This work does not replace medical care, psychiatric treatment, or psychotherapy. If you are in crisis or need clinical care, please reach a qualified medical professional or local emergency services first.

Questions about privacy or scope can be raised directly with Vinay before your call.
