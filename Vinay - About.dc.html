Why this matters to me

My path.

Checking out was once my default. For a long time, I coped by making my world smaller. I disappeared into games, drugs and other forms of escape. By 24, I had dropped out of university and had almost no trust in myself.

I had searched for relief, but the misery underneath remained. In that state, I turned to something from my childhood that I had long dismissed: pranayama and meditation. That experience opened a door into years of healing, transformation, personal development and spiritual practice.

Relationships and responsibility took the work further. Ordinary life revealed layers of avoidance that no session could fully reach. Today, blind spots and overwhelm still arise. What has changed is the trust I carry into them. I trust my capacity to digest what comes, find a way through and do what is needed.

The Dream

I dream of a tipping point where enough of the people shaping our world are willing to face what is difficult. Within themselves. Within the systems they lead.

What we refuse to face does not disappear. Its cost is carried somewhere, often by other people, other species or those who come after us. As more becomes visible, the frame widens to include human lives, other species, ecosystems, and generations we will never meet.

I envision a world where power serves life. If this is a future you want to help shape, how you meet your own life is part of the work.
