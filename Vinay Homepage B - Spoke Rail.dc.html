The Invitation

When we feel welcome, we relax. We open up. It becomes easier to connect and contribute.

If we offer life the same welcome, it opens up more of its beauty, depth, and mystery.

Practically, this looks like finding the steadiness to stay with the difficult moments, so you can respond to life with choice rather than reflex.
